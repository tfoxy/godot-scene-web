## G8f result (2026-10-23)

Float texture payloads now travel end to end at render-stream/8 (gate8-design.md D9): the capture
carries all eight float formats (`RF RGF RGBF RGBAF RH RGH RGBH RGBAH`), decodes BC6H and ASTC-HDR to
half floats, and the Rust core hands them to the native receivers exact or quantizes them to RGBA8
for a receiver that cannot sample float. Wire and receiver facts: render-stream-8.md "As built
(G8f)"; design choices: gate8-design.md Q3 "As built (G8f)".

### What changed

- **Capture** (`rs_texture_payload.cpp`, `rs_texture_decode.cpp`, `hooks.cpp`): the size table for
  Image.Format 8-15, so `GRC_RESOURCE_FORMATS` parses the float names; the decode table gains
  BPTC_RGBF/RGBFU -> RGBH and ASTC_4x4_HDR/ASTC_8x8_HDR -> RGBAH; a decode whose payload the policy
  would refuse is not run; `perturb-decode` inverts a float texel's whole first channel. **The
  default format list stays the six 8-bit formats** (floats are opt-in, and still need a /8 sink).
- **rs-core / rs-core-capi**: `verify_decode`, the capi's `verify` and its `Payloads` take the
  stream version (float payloads legal at /8, `resource-payload` below it); `image_format` codes
  8-15; `texture_rgba8` converts every float format.
- **Quantization rule** (what GSW takes; `float-texture-quantized` is the receiver's caveat): channels
  map like the 8-bit family (R -> (r,0,0,1), RG -> (r,g,0,1), RGB -> (r,g,b,1), RGBA -> (r,g,b,a));
  halves widen exactly to binary32; each channel is 0 for NaN, else `floor(fl32(fl32(clamp(v,0,1) *
  255) + 0.5))` (round half up, single precision, no sRGB, straight alpha, level 0 only).
  `protocol/golden-8/float-quantize.json` holds vectors for all eight formats, generated by the
  checker's independent TypeScript implementation and asserted byte for byte by rs-core's tests.
- **Native receiver** (`rs_core_ext.cpp`): no logic change (`make_image` already passes the core's
  format code to `Image.create_from_data`); a comment records the 8-15 codes.
- **Fixture and checks** (`fixtures/gate8-tex/gate8_float.{gd,tscn}`, `expected-float.json`,
  `lib/gate8-float-checks.ts`, `g8f` group in `run-gate8.sh`, `test/self-test-gate8f.ts`): STS2's
  case. Engine `CurveTexture` (RED and RGB), `CurveXYZTexture` and an HDR `GradientTexture1D`
  (256x1, deferred updates), seven float images of the other formats (one with a 5-level mip chain,
  one 5x3 odd-sized) built and uploaded on a loader `Thread`, and the imported EXR's BC6H and ASTC-HDR
  decodes; every texture is the `src` uniform of a canvas_item shader on a `ColorRect`, four are also
  sprites. Step 1 changes the curves (`texture_2d_update`), re-creates the gradient at width 192
  (`texture_replace`) and `ImageTexture.update`s two images.

### Out of scope (skipped_targets)

- The timing phase: native gate 6, `g7live-*-timing`, `g7x`, and the timing groups of the native
  gates (g1c-rate, g1d, g2c-pins, g5f, g55f, g8t's `decode-cost`). They are deferred to the
  pre-release delta sweep; nothing here changes a rate or a pacing path, and the capture's only
  default-path change is a table lookup.
- The browser receivers' float checks (GSW quantization seen in a browser, web-export float
  upload): G8g. rs-core's quantization is what they take, and the golden vectors pin it; the
  design's "GSW within its band" half of `float-exact` therefore belongs to G8g.
- `packages/canvas` was not touched (no `Changelog:` line, no consumer check).

### Deviations from the design

- `float-exact` is the native receivers against the reference, exact; GSW's band is G8g's (above).
- The reference's HDR textures come from a headless run's `Image.decompress` dump
  (`RS_FIXTURE_HDR_DUMP`), not from a rendered process: a rendered GLES3 run's
  `CompressedTexture2D.get_image()` is the GPU path's image, not the creation-time one (ASTC HDR is
  not sampleable on desktop and came back empty, 4,608 px of difference on the first run).
- The design's `GRC_RESOURCE_FORMATS` list is opt-in; no float default was added.
- The `float-census` "two threads" criterion is the design's "mostly from loader threads" case.

### Time

Implementation (capture, rs-core, capi, fixture, checker, docs): about 2.5 h of work. Validation:
the g8f legs were 3.5 min of runner wall time on a quiet lock but ~17 min and ~16 min on the two
runs that had to queue behind other agents' exclusive timing legs; the remote full tier took
about 2 h 15 min from dispatch to collection; the local live g7-1/g7-2 runs queue behind the same
exclusive legs, hours of mostly waiting. Total wall time was well over the ~4.5 h budget, nearly all
of it lock waits.
