## Gate 8k9 result (2026-10-24)

G8k9 ([protocol/texture-delivery-design.md](../../protocol/texture-delivery-design.md) "G8k9") passes:
render-stream/9, the texture-delivery wire, is implemented as a protocol-version switch on the same
modules /3 to /8 extended (memory g4e1-rs3-version-switch-pattern), with its own document
([protocol/render-stream-9.md](../../protocol/render-stream-9.md), "As built (G8k9)") and
`protocol/golden-9/`. **/9 is opt-in and nothing offers it**: codecs and validators only. The
listener offers `render-stream.9` only under `GRC_STREAM_VERSION=9`, no receiver's hello asks for it,
and the delivery seam offers no variant, so every /2 to /8 byte is unchanged.

### What /9 adds (Q5, nothing more)

- magic `GRS9`, `render-stream/9`, subprotocol `render-stream.9`;
- session key `resources.texture_delivery` `{mode, encoder}` after `resources.decode` (the design
  said `resources.delivery`, which /2 already uses: renamed, see the doc's "As built");
- texture-entry `variants` (sorted `block`, `uastc`, `etc1s`; at most one each; `[]` unless an ok
  image) right after `payload_bytes`; invariant code `delivery-entry`;
- `block` GRT1 payloads with Godot's block sizes (legal only as a variant) and the
  `render-stream-texture/ktx2` payload with header, level-index and DFD validation (`ktx2-header`,
  `ktx2-shape`);
- the texture reason `delivery-pending`; the hello keys `texture_encodings` and `gpu_formats`;
- `texture-version` relaxed in one way: `variants` may grow at an equal version.

Capture: `GRC_STREAM_VERSION=6|8|9`, `GRC_TEXTURE_DELIVERY=rgba|passthrough|basis-uastc|basis-etc1s`
(declared by a /9 session, ignored below). The empty seam `rs_texture_delivery.{h,cpp}` is called from
`entry.cpp` (the declaration, kept in the `ResourcePolicy`) and `Mirror::wire_entry` (the variants).

### Evidence

- **Independent oracles agree.** `protocol/golden-9/` (`make_golden.py`, stdlib only; 68 outputs):
  the full/patch/inline triple (eight states, four variants, a variant appearing at an equal
  version, a `delivery-pending` entry), the off leg, 26 invalid vectors and 14 control vectors.
  The C++ encoder (`rs9_codec_test`) matches every `.rs9` byte for byte; the TypeScript decoder
  (`self-test-rs2.ts`, 900 checks) and rs-core (`rs-core-check goldens`: golden-9 138/138,
  cross-version 12/12; `cargo test` 51) decode, validate and resolve them and reject every invalid
  vector with exactly its code. `golden-2` to `golden-8` are unchanged (`make_golden.py --check`
  passes for each).
- **KTX2 sources.** Two real UASTC LDR 4x4 + Zstandard files written by Godot 4.5.1's editor binary
  from procedural images (`golden-9/gen_ktx2.gd`), committed; synthetic, no game content.
- **A real /9 capture** (gate 8 `delivery9/capture`, `GRC_STREAM_VERSION=9
  GRC_TEXTURE_DELIVERY=basis-uastc` over the g8t fixture) writes full and patch recordings that
  start GRS9, validate at /9, declare `{basis-uastc, null}`, carry `variants: []` on all 420 texture
  entries, and equal the /8 capture of the same fixture state for state once that key is stripped
  (check `delivery-v9-wire`; it found the bug that the declaration was lost in
  `Publisher::start`, fixed before landing).
- **The listener** offers `.9` only under `GRC_STREAM_VERSION=9` (`rs_gate8_policy_test`), a /9
  connection gets a /9 session beside /6, /7 and /8 connections on one hub, and `/8`-hello on `/9` is
  `error protocol` close 1002 (`rs_live_test` `test_v9_connection`).

### Out of scope here (and who owns it)

- **Receivers:** the native receiver (`receiver.gd`/`rs_wire.gd` know GRS8 at most; its hello offers
  `.6/.7`), GSW (`web/gsw-receiver`, `web/gsw-rs`) and the web export are untouched; none offers `.9`.
  The capi accepts `version` 9 so a `.rs9` recording replays as /8 would. G8kp (native and GSW block
  upload, `texture_encodings`/`gpu_formats` filled from hardware), G8ku (native/web-export transcode),
  G8kb (GSW transcoder), G8ke.
- **Gate 7 groups** (`g7-*`, `g7live-*`) and the device legs reach the change only through shared
  capture files; none sets `GRC_STREAM_VERSION=9`. They run in the full tier for regression, not for
  /9. **Timing groups** (native gate 6, `g7live-*-timing`, `g7x`) are deferred to the pre-release
  delta sweep.
- **The encoder, the host cache, lazy variants, `delivery-pending` in a capture**, `transcode-oracle`,
  `delivery-reference`, `quality-band` and `perturb-ktx2`: G8ku and later; the seam is empty.
- **K3**: warming the cache from a pack is a deferred library feature (design doc, "Deferred, with
  owners"); K1 (basisu 1.60) and K2 (ETC1S does not gate the release) are recorded as decided.
