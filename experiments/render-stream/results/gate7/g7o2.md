## Gate 7o2 result: the Rust core's step-transaction cost on the g31

Design: [protocol/gate7/g7o2.md](../../protocol/gate7/g7o2.md).

**On the g31, three of GSW's four bench inputs passed before G7o2, and they still pass. hd-patch
still misses, but its p95 dropped from 13.14 to 10.64 ms against 0.5·T_p = 8.51 ms. On the Rust
core, the web export's scene-full now passes: p95 10.05 → 7.49 ms. Its hd-patch still misses, at
13.18 → 12.35 ms.** Both remaining misses are the typed finding `device-decode-over-budget`, with
the per-phase numbers below. No budget moved.

Two changes did it:

- GSW verifies and decodes resource payloads when they arrive, not in the frame that uses them.
- The web export defers the record digest (D31).

The applier's dependency re-lower and its micro-optimizations made non-step frames much cheaper.
On their own they did not move the p95 (see "Why the first cuts did not move the p95").

Evidence (ignored): `artifacts/render-stream/gate7/g7o2/` in the worktree. The final benches are
`g31-final-gsw/` and `g31-final-web/`. The re-runs are under `runs/`.

### g31, D32 protocol

There are four inputs, one warmup and five repeats each. GSW interleaves `gsw-rs-base` (`main`
`bce688ac`'s build, `rs-core/bce688acb34f`) with `gsw-rs` (this tree), round by round. The web
export runs `main`'s `Web (rust core)` bundle (`fe4066e241fbb2cc`) and this tree's bundle back to
back in every warmup and repeat slot. Judgment is p95 against 0.5·T_p, with T_p measured
(17.01 ms, 0.5·T_p = 8.51 ms).

Conditions, per page: thermal status `none` throughout, maximum sensor 45.5 °C, battery at 100 %.
The A75 cores ran at 1354–2000 MHz in both arms, and the A55s at 1500–1800 MHz. The governor's
derating was alike in the before and after arms (GSW: 70 and 68 derated samples out of 96).

The p95 over 5 × 122 samples is about the sixth-worst step transaction per page. The payload work
has left those frames. What remains is apply, at 4–8 ms, and decode, at 2–6 ms, on a phone that
clocks its A75s between 1.35 and 2.0 GHz.

The phone profile (`g31-prof-*`, timers on) splits a step transaction's apply as follows. In
seq 71 it was walk 2.4, lower (text re-lower) 1.9, classify 1.5 and assemble 1.4 ms. Natively the
same transaction takes 250–800 µs: the phone is 13–40 times slower on pointer-heavy phases.

**The next bounded item** is the step transaction's apply and resolve: text re-lower, classify and
the walk's resolve/reindex, cut natively first (`rs-core-check profile`), since native cuts carry
over roughly proportionally. The web export's apply (hd-patch 4.5 / 9.5 ms on the g31) is
unprofiled. Profiling it needs timers in the capi planner, the C++ op execution and the GDScript
marshalling. The web export's payload `provide` (capi hash and copy, decoded at apply in
`plan.rs`) can move to receiver.gd's fetch completion in the same way as GSW's.

### Why the first cuts did not move the p95

The dependency re-lower in place, the G7a moves fix and the applier micro-optimizations
(`g31-m-noprof/`, three interleaved rounds) made non-step frames much cheaper:

- seq 69: 11.85 → 4.95 ms;
- p50: 6.69 → 5.96 ms;
- apply p50: 3.40 → 1.89 ms.

The p95 went from 13.0 to 13.5 ms. The untouched phases (decode, provide and draw: the same code in
both arms) ran about 30 % slower in the after arm. With less work per frame the phone clocks down,
or schedules the main thread on an A55, and a burst then runs slower. **A cut to steady-state work
does not lower the g31's p95. Only cheaper step transactions do.** Payload verification ahead of use
was the first change aimed at them.

### Deviations

- **Bench fetch completion.** The bench's fetcher holds every payload already, so its completion
  (verify and decode, `payload_verify_us`) runs synchronously in the same rAF callback, between
  accept and provide. It is timed outside `total_us`, in the same way as D31's `evidence_hash_us`
  is timed after the frame. The receiver page runs it in the fetch's own completion task.
- **One wrong web run, discarded.** The first web-rust g31 run deferred the digest only for logged
  transactions. Under the bench's `RS_RECEIVER_LOG=summary`, no digest was taken at all. That is
  skipping, which D31 forbids. receiver.gd now digests every accepted record after the frame
  whatever the log mode, `_finish` checks the count, and the numbers above are from the corrected
  run.
- **No web-export digest sabotage leg.** `record-digest-incomplete` is checked at `_finish`. No
  `RS_RECEIVER_SABOTAGE` leg drops a web digest.
- **Priority 3 was not done:** text re-lower and resolve cuts in step transactions, and web-export
  apply profiling. It is recorded above as the next bounded item.
