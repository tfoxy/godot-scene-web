# W13c: GSW and the render-stream client accept render-stream/13 (2026-10-29)

W13c makes the GSW canvas receiver and the private live client (`packages/render-stream-client`,
MIR-1) accept render-stream/13 as W13a built it and W13b's capture emits it: /12 plus `GRSD`,
`predict_check` and the predicted `mesh_u8`/`mesh_f32`/`mm_f32` under /12's JSON meta (the binary
body stays on hold). File mode reads a `GRSD` recording by its magic; live mode offers
`render-stream.13` under `RS_RECEIVER_PROTOCOL=13`, or `protocol: 13` in the client's endpoint (12
stays what a product passes). "As built (W13c): GSW" in
[render-stream-13.md](../../protocol/render-stream-13.md) is the spec of what was built.

## What changed

**GSW module** (`web/gsw-rs/src/lib.rs`).

- `live_protocol` 13 in the page's options; `live_session` then takes `GRSD` only.
- The dynamic-mesh and multimesh ingest read `Validator::record_view(record)`: at /13 the
  reconstructed /12 record whose ranges `dynamic_bodies()` and `multimesh_buffers()` index (W13a's
  note). Nothing else in the module reads a transaction's block bytes.
- `predict-check` on a live connection: the validator changes nothing on an error; the module
  counts it, sets `predict_await_full` and returns the error. Until a full transaction arrives,
  `live_accept` drops each patch without accepting it (`peek_transaction` reads the meta's
  canonical prefix: `{"type":"transaction","seq":N,"frame":N,"encoding":"…"`) and returns the new
  code `ACCEPT_HELD` (4). A new connection (`live_reset`) clears it.
- The receiver sabotage `no-predict-reset` (`RS_RECEIVER_SABOTAGE`): rs-core's
  `Options::no_predict_reset`.
- `evidence_json` gains `w13` at /13: `predict_failures`, `predict_dropped`, `no_predict_reset`,
  `history_units` (H1's units at the end).

**GSW page** (`web/gsw-receiver/`). `config.ts` takes `RS_RECEIVER_PROTOCOL=13` (live coding as
at /11 and /12); `live.ts` sends /12's hello under `render-stream/13`, turns a `predict-check` error
into W11t's `codingResync` (reason `coding`, GTS's `transport_skip`, a rebuilt applier for the
full; the seq from the coding envelope, or the plain message's meta), ignores `ACCEPT_HELD`
messages (no apply, no ack) and writes `live.predict` (`resyncs` and the module's `w13`); `main.ts`
reports a `GRSD` recording as `render-stream/13`.

**Client** (`packages/render-stream-client`, private). `RenderStreamProtocol` and
`validateEndpoint` take 13; `clientHello` names `render-stream/${protocol}`; `abi.ts` exports
`ACCEPT_HELD`; `core.ts` returns a `predict-check` failure as a `coding-resync` message (the loop
already resyncs those) and `ACCEPT_HELD` as `held`. Tests: endpoint 13 accepted and 14 refused,
the /13 hello equal to /12's but for `protocol`. The default stays 12: the endpoint has no default,
and couch-coop's seat says `GRC_STREAM_VERSION=12` (W13z switches the product).

**Harness.**

- `gate8-w11-tool.ts`: GSW-only g8w11 legs on W13b's captures, `scale13` and `mesh13` (capture-
  scale's patch sink, gate5-mesh's full sink) with their /12 twins and
  `sabotage-no-predict-reset/mesh13`; mesh13's shot frames are also the /11 mesh leg's, so its
  shots meet the native receiver's. `native-plan` leaves them out until W13d. `--set pt13` replays
  g8pt's headless /13 twins (`headless-pump13`: gate8-mm's multimesh buffers under the
  frame_pre_draw pump, shot at g8mm's settle frames; `headless-shim13`: gate8-pt's particles) and
  their /12 twins under `g8pt/gsw13/`, called by run_g8pt after its captures.
- `run-gate8.sh` `run_g8w11c_gsw13` (g8w11c, GSW only under `GATE7_LIVE_KIND=gsw`): a /13
  capture-scale host (every mesh every frame) and one coded GSW receiver at /13 that shoots its
  final state: `gsw13-honest`, `gsw13-predict-stale` (host predict-stale @120) and
  `gsw13-sabotage-no-predict-reset`. `run_g8w11_coding_native_leg` takes the receiver's protocol
  from `G8W11C_PROTOCOL` (default 11).
- `lib/gate8-w13-gsw-checks.ts`, the checks below; `lib/gate7-web-legs.ts` names a `.rs13` copy.

In the honest resync leg no patch reached the module between the failure and the forced full

require a drop.

/13 shots with the native receiver's /11 shots of the same fixture frames, not with a native /13
leg. g8mm's own GSW legs replay its /11 captures, which do not transcode to /13 (`transcode` is
/12 ↔ /13), so the multimesh check runs on g8pt's headless gate8-mm capture at /13, W13b's
`headless-pump13`, against g8mm's reference. gate8-pt's rendered capture has no /13 twin either;
its GSW check at /13 is equality with the /12 twin of the headless shim capture.

## Cost

`profile-rs-core-wasm.mjs` (rs-core's `prof::replay` in wasm in Node, every timer's minimum over 5
repeats, under `quiet.sh exclusive`) on pm7-a12-1 (STS2 menu, map and combat, 4,955 transactions)
and its /13 transcode (`rs-core-check transcode --to 13`, 281,058,616 → 281,232,041 bytes):

| per transaction | /12 | /13 | change |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| `accept_us` p50 | 216.7 µs | 265.8 µs | +49.1 µs, **+22.7 %** |
| `accept_us` p95 | 224.4 | 275.8 | +22.9 % |
| `accept_us` mean | 143.6 | 174.6 | +21.6 % |
| `pre_checks` p50 (un-predict, check, plain record) | 3.6 | 42.0 | +38.4 |
| core total p50 (accept, prepare, apply, digest; no draw) | 608.5 | 659.3 | **+8.4 %** |

After rebasing onto MRA (`ed671b61`, delta-driven material residency: apply, not accept) the same
profile (load 8.9 outside the repo's lock) gave accept p50 216.3 → 266.1 µs (+23.0 %) and total p50
584.8 → 635.0 µs (+8.6 %).

The design's +10 % on accept is **missed**. An instrumented build split the /13 extra in wasm into
33.5 µs un-predicting the blocks (`code_blocks`), 9.2 advancing the history, 2.9 the XXH64 and 1.4
copying the plain record: 2.6× native's 13 / 5 / 2.7 / 2 (W13a), a usual wasm ratio, so no
wasm-specific defect. A `+simd128` build (LLVM's autovectorization only) took `pre_checks` to 30.1
and accept to +17.4 %; still over, and a module-wide target-feature change that is not this
increment's to make. Reaching +10 % (≤ +21.7 µs) needs both SIMD and a zero-copy history (H1/H2 as
ranges of retained plain records instead of copied bodies): a perf increment of its own if the g31
numbers ask for it. The design's own projection ("/13 is a network change, not a receiver
speed-up"; ±0.2 ms a transaction on the g31) stands: about +0.5 ms on the g31 at ~11× desktop wasm.
Evidence: `artifacts/render-stream/p3/w13c/core-profile-summary.json` and the per-transaction rows.

## Notes for W13d and W13z

- The native receiver (capi) needs what GSW needed: `record_view` at its ingest, a
  `predict-check` → `coding` resync that drops the patches before the full (GSW's `ACCEPT_HELD`
  peeks the meta's canonical prefix; the capi has its own accept codes), and `no-predict-reset`
  plumbed. Then drop `gswOnly` from the g8w11 /13 legs in `gate8-w11-tool.ts` so `native-plan`
  plans them, and run `run_g8w11c_gsw13`'s legs for the native receiver too.
- The client's `protocol: 13` is ready; W13z switches couch-coop's seat (`GRC_STREAM_VERSION=13`)
  and its endpoint together.
- If the g31 asks for the receiver cost back, the levers are measured above (SIMD −12 µs desktop
  wasm, the history copy 9 µs).
