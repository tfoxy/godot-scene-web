# W11a: render-stream/11 codecs, goldens and validators (2026-10-27)

W11a builds render-stream/11 ([protocol/render-stream-11.md](../../protocol/render-stream-11.md))
at the wire level, without the capture or the receivers. Three codecs implement it and match
`protocol/golden-11/` byte for byte or verdict for verdict:

- the C++ encoder (`rs2_codec`, `rs2_diff`);
- the TypeScript oracle (`render-stream-2.ts`);
- rs-core (`validate.rs` and its neighbours).

What they check:

- the pruned carried set;
- dynamic mesh surfaces by id (`u8`, `mesh_u8`);
- the empty `mm_f32` slot;
- `resources.mesh_dynamic`;
- WB's stable `content_version` and patched `unsupported`.

/11 is selected only by its version. Every /2–/10 path is unchanged: every `make_golden.py --check`
passes, and every earlier golden suite in every codec passes untouched.

The capture's classification and live wiring are W11b. The receivers' retention and uploads are
W11c to W11e. The multimesh table is PM1, and transport compression is W11t.

## Decisions

The design left some choices open and got a few details wrong. W11a decided them on long-term
(frozen-wire) merit and wrote them into render-stream-11.md's new "As built (W11a): decisions":

1. **The golden scene's six states** differ in detail from the sketch:
   - mesh 1, static since state 4, turns dynamic at 11 (the static-to-dynamic transition in a
     patch);
   - mesh 2 is new and dynamic with two surfaces, so the offset rule runs over two entries;
   - texture 5's hash leaves at 12 and returns at 13;
   - mesh 2's bytes change at 13;
   - mesh 1's shape changes at 14;
   - mesh 2 is freed at 15;
   - `unsupported` changes at 14 and 15, so every other patch carries `null`.
2. **Record order** is table order: textures by id (hash, then variants), meshes, shaders, first
   occurrence. That makes the records a function of (B, T). The C++ planner leaves variants out
   of inline(S) by default (D12(c)). Only the golden turns them on.
3. **Multimesh before PM1.** The ninth block `mm_f32` is always present and must be empty
   (`mm-offset`). `features` stays /10's until PM1 plans the hooks.
4. **Check order** inside a transaction: `mesh-dynamic`, then `mesh-u8-offset`, then
   `payload-size`. Each invalid vector fails one rule only.
5. **The resolved surface** gains `body_sha256` after `hash`. A resolved mesh has no `u8`.
6. **`resource-unnamed`** covers the records since the previous transaction, at the next
   transaction or at the end record.
7. **`content-version-stable`** compares against the base's resolved commands, with floats by
   float32 bits. A full transaction is exempt.
8. **`mesh-dynamic-policy`** covers the object's keys and values. A missing key is `meta-schema`.
9. **`inline-fetch-zero`** is a validator predicate: rs-core `Validator::check_fetch`, TypeScript
   `checkInlineFetch`. It refuses a GET for a non-variant name of the newest state with
   `payload_bytes ≤ inline_max_bytes`, or for any hash in C. W11c and W11d call it from their
   fetchers.
10. **Sabotages.**
    - `keep-carried` (a C++ codec flag) writes exactly `invalid/resource-missing.rs11`, and every
      validator rejects that file at seq 13.
    - `early-prune` (a validator flag in rs-core and TypeScript) prunes against the base's names
      before the checks, so `inline.rs11` fails `resource-missing` at seq 1. The sketch's "on state
      12" assumed a prune against S itself, which can never fail, because required(S) ⊆ names(S).
11. **`resource-duplicate-v10`** is a /10 file in golden-11's `invalid/`. Every invalid index entry
    carries its `version`.
12. **rs-core-capi** accepts /11 (`VERSIONS` gains 11). Its planner leaves dynamic meshes undrawn:
    their `add_mesh` is typed `mesh-dynamic`, and the `meshes_dynamic_undrawn` stat is written
    only when it is non-zero. The live hello offers no `.11`.
13. **`rs_wire.gd`** is untouched. Its magic table gains `B` with W11d's offer.

## What changed

**Goldens** (`protocol/golden-11/`, `make_golden.py --check`: 36 outputs). The generator loads
golden-10's and re-binds what changes.

- **Valid files.**
  - `full.rs11`, `patch.rs11` and `inline.rs11`, with their `.hex` and `.decoded.json`, and
    `resolved.json`.
  - All three hold 16 states: golden-10's ten plus the six above.
  - `patch.rs11`'s fulls are seqs 1, 5 and 16.
  - `inline.rs11` re-carries F before seq 13, and seq 16 (a resync) has no records.
- **Index.** `index.json` lists:
  - per-seq `names`;
  - `inline_resources` with `before_seq`;
  - three `dynamic_bodies` (`payloads/*.body`).
- **Invalid vectors (19).**
  - `bad-magic`;
  - `resource-missing` (keep-carried's output), `resource-duplicate`, `resource-duplicate-v10`
    (/10), `resource-unnamed` and `resource-unnamed-end`;
  - `mesh-dynamic`, four ways: `-mixed`, `-full-null`, `-static-u8`, `-shape-null`;
  - `mesh-u8-offset` and `mesh-u8-offset-short`;
  - `payload-size`;
  - `meta-schema`, twice: `mm-f32-type` and `unsupported-null-full`;
  - `mm-offset`;
  - `mesh-dynamic-policy`, twice: mode and frames;
  - `content-version-stable` (the shape of the capture sabotage `cv-churn`).
- **Other files.** `corrupt-meta.rs11` and two control hellos.

**C++** (`capture/`).

- **Protocol.** `ProtocolVersion::V11` and `is_v10_or_later()`.
- **Session.** `ResourcesInfo::mesh_dynamic` (`MeshDynamicPolicy`, default auto/4/65536).
- **Snapshot.**
  - `MeshEntry::dynamic` and `MeshEntry::content_serial`;
  - `MeshSurface::body`;
  - `Transaction::mesh_bodies_held` and `Transaction::unsupported_as_base`.
- **`make_patch`** sets `u8` from (B, T) serials and shapes, and writes `unsupported: null`.
- **Records.** `records_before(base, target, InlinePolicy)` gives inline(T) \ inline(B) in table
  order. `RecordPlanner(version, policy, keep_carried)` wraps it for the sabotage.
- **Tests.** New `test/rs11_codec_test.cpp` (ctest `rs11_codec`), plus
  `rs_live_test`'s `test_records_from_base_and_target`. The live connection and `rs_carried` are
  untouched; W11b wires them.

**TypeScript** (`scripts/lib/render-stream-2.ts`, `scripts/test/self-test-rs2.ts`).

- `version` widens to 11. The session, block, mesh, carried-set and WB rules are in place, and
  `checkDynamicMeshes` holds the mesh ones.
- `resolvedStateNames` (names(S)).
- `RecordingValidator.checkInlineFetch` and the exported `checkInlineFetch`.
- The `early-prune` option.
- `runSuite(golden-11, 11)`, plus cross-version `bad-magic` with golden-10 and `checkHello11`.

**Rust** (`rs-core`, `rs-core-capi`, `web/gsw-rs` only to keep it compiling).

- **Data model.**
  - `MeshSurface.hash` is now `Option<Hash>`, with `body_sha256`.
  - `Mesh::is_dynamic()`.
  - `payload::mesh_body_bytes` and `payload::decode_mesh_body`.
- **Validator.**
  - It prunes by reference counts of names(S), updated from each transaction's undo log, so a
    transaction costs O(delta). The upkeep is off below /11.
  - Results: `pruned()`, `released()` (the hashes that left C ∪ names(S_n)) and
    `dynamic_bodies()` (byte ranges inside the accepted record).
  - `check_fetch`, plus `Options.early_prune` and `GoldenOptions.early_prune`.
  - `names_in_table_order`.
- **Goldens.** `goldens.rs` runs golden-11 (111 checks) and the 10↔11 `bad-magic` pairs.
  `rs-core-check goldens --sabotage early-prune` proves the sabotage.
- **Property tests.** The new `carried_tests.rs` has three, over 24 seeds × 2 sessions: 9,852
  transactions, 6,991 records, 3,262 prunes, 1,281 re-carried hashes and 894 null-`u8` re-sends.
  - **L2.** Random state sequences with resyncs, folds and window 2, through a small Rust test
    encoder (xorshift, no new crates). C equals inline(S) after every transaction.
  - **L1.** A hub serves N connections from different bases. Each connection's validator accepts
    its records.

  With the prune disabled, the tests fail.
- **Applier.** The rs-core applier counts dynamic meshes as `MeshResidency.dynamic_undrawn` and
  does not draw them (W11c).

## Deviations

- **Golden states** differ in detail from the sketch (decision 1), and **`early-prune`** fails at
  seq 1 rather than at state 12 (decision 10).
- **No `cv-churn` sabotage run.** It is a capture sabotage, so it lands with W11b's stable
  `content_version`. W11a ships the invariant, and the `content-version-stable` vector in the
  sabotage's shape.
- **No GDScript codec change** (decision 13).
- **`carried-bounded` and `residency-bounded`.** Gate 6's /11 versions of these TypeScript checkers
  need /11 legs, so they come with W11c and W11d.
- **Implementation was split** between three parallel agents (TypeScript, Rust, C++) against the
  same frozen golden, written first. Implementation took about 1 h of agent time; validation took
  about 2 h of wall time.

## For W11b and W11c

- **W11b (capture).** These are already built and need wiring:
  - `MeshDynamicPolicy` (the env vars);
  - `MeshEntry::dynamic` and `content_serial` from the mirror;
  - `MeshSurface::body`;
  - `RecordPlanner` per /11 connection, instead of `rs_carried`;
  - the listener's protocol table (`rs_live.cpp` has no V11 entry yet).

  `rs_captured.h`'s `payload_refs` still lists dynamic surfaces, and must skip them. The capture's
  stable `content_version` and `cv-churn` are W11b's: `invalid/content-version-stable.rs11` shows
  the shape the validators reject.
- **W11c (GSW).**
  - Retention reads `Validator::pruned()`/`released()`, plus names(S_d) held by the receiver.
  - Uploads read `dynamic_bodies()`.
  - The fetcher calls `check_fetch`.
  - `meta/fast.rs` falls back to the general path for /11 patches that carry meshes, which may
    show in the combat parse cost.
- **TypeScript typing.** `TransactionMeshSurface.hash` and `TransactionMeta.unsupported` stay
  typed non-null, though /11 can carry null. Widening them adds about 40 tsc errors in older gate
  checkers, so a /11 consumer should use `surfaceIsDynamic()` and the resolved form.
